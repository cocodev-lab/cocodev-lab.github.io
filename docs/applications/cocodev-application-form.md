# Apply to CoCoDev

Tell us about the opportunity you are interested in and the questions you would like to work on.

## Full name
- Field type: short text
- Required: yes

## Email address
- Field type: email
- Required: yes

## Position you are interested in
- Field type: dropdown, one selection
- Required: yes
- Options:
  - PhD
  - Research engineer
  - Postdoctoral research
  - Master’s project or internship
  - Research visit

## Current institution or employer
- Field type: short text
- Required: no

## Why would you like to join CoCoDev, and what would you ideally like to work on?
- Field type: long text
- Required: yes
- Help text: About 300 words. Describe the questions you would like to investigate, why they interest you, and how they connect with CoCoDev’s research.
- Do not enforce a word limit.

## Earliest start date and availability
- Field type: short text
- Required: no
- Help text: Include your preferred start date and, for a project or visit, its proposed duration.

## CV
- Field type: file upload
- Required: yes
- Accept one PDF file, up to 10 MB.

## Recommender’s name
- Field type: short text
- Required: yes
- Help text: An academic or professional referee familiar with your work.

## Recommender’s email address
- Field type: email
- Required: yes
